1.1 - Answering Machine now has profiles with their own greeting, ring delay, Do Not Disturb choice and call duration; pick a default with a radio button. Voice lists are searchable. Calling Machine profiles place scheduled calls with their own greetings or from Tasker, and tag their recordings. Tasker calling and answering actions can use a saved profile from a dropdown. Greetings can be audio files from storage, with a delay in milliseconds (0 = immediately).
