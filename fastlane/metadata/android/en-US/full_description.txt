A call recorder for unrooted Android — phone and messenger calls. One APK. No root, no companion app, no computer: the phone sets itself up with Wireless debugging, or optionally with Shizuku.

<b>How it works.</b> Android's voice-call source carries both sides, but only shell identities may read it. The app embeds an ADB client and pairs with the phone's own Wireless debugging over 127.0.0.1. Nothing leaves the phone.

A short ADB session grants WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS, allow-lists a notification listener if asked, and starts a Java daemon from a bundled jar. The session closes; the daemon outlives it.

<b>Recordings</b> are Ogg Opus, about 12 MB/hour, in Recordings/AM Call Assistant. Automatic records every call; ask-first waits for your tap. Messenger calls (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Viber) and Google Meet on Android 14+. Warns of silent or cut-short recordings and settings in the way.

<b>Playing, finding, keeping.</b> Waveform playback, both ears, earpiece, screen off. A Calls tab shows each call, or why not. Recently deleted keeps files 30 days; auto-delete clears old and short ones. Keep only some people's calls.

<b>Three looks</b>: Memo, Deck, Multitrack - light, dark or as the phone.

<b>Tasker</b>: three Plugin &gt; AM Call Assistant actions - Toggle, Answering Machine and Calling Machine. Number fields offer your contacts in a dropdown: type to filter, tap to fill. App defaults and Revert.

<b>Answering machine</b>, off until enabled: plays your recorded, typed or audio-file greeting with the microphone muted and keeps the caller's message. Experimental; Diagnostics has a self-test.

<b>Calling Machine</b>: profiles place calls at a chosen time or from Tasker and play that profile's greeting.

<b>Setup</b>: turn on Developer options, USB and Wireless debugging, then pair once. Fresh installs can pick Shizuku instead (installed, running, allowing the app; no pairing code). Settings &gt; Setup &gt; Mode of operation shows the mode and switches it, keeping recordings, permissions and pairing. Pairing survives reboots and Android's 7-day limit if you let it; the recorder restarts on Wi-Fi if the phone allows it.

<b>What stays on.</b> USB and Wireless debugging stay on while the app is on; a paired computer can connect on the same Wi-Fi. Start without Wi-Fi opens port 5555 until a reboot.

<b>Requirements.</b> Android 12+, and a phone whose audio hardware exposes the voice-call source. Both sides recorded on Honor Magic 8 Pro, Galaxy S20, S23, M34 and a OnePlus on LineageOS.

<b>Recording calls is your responsibility.</b> Consent law differs by country and state; the app warns nobody for you.

<b>Not asked for:</b> RECORD_AUDIO or phone state. INTERNET is for the loopback socket and update check. READ_MEDIA_AUDIO lists old recordings. READ_CALL_LOG and READ_CONTACTS are optional, to name callers. MANAGE_ONGOING_CALLS detects call end. Notification access hides the debugging notification or names a messenger caller.
