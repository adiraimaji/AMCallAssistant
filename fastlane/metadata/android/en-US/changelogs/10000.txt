1.0 - first release of AM Call Assistant. Records phone and messenger calls on unrooted Android with no companion app. Includes the AM Answering Machine, two Tasker actions (Toggle AM Call Assistant and AM Answering Machine), Tasker variables in greetings, a longest-message field in seconds, and an in-app updater that shows the release notes before installing. Release APK: AM-Call-Assistant.apk.
