1.1 - Answering Machine now has profiles: save several setups with their own greeting, ring delay, Do Not Disturb choice and call duration, and pick a default with a radio button. Voice lists in the app and Tasker are searchable, and a tap picks a voice. Calling Machine profiles place scheduled calls, keep their own greetings, and can be set to Tasker trigger so only a Tasker action starts them; calls they place are tagged in the recordings list. Both Tasker calling and answering actions can use a saved profile from a dropdown, with Custom for typed fields. Greetings can be audio files from storage. The greeting delay is settable in milliseconds in both the app profile and the Tasker action: 0 plays immediately, and the default is 1000 ms.
