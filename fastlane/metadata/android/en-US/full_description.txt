A call recorder for unrooted Android — phone and messenger calls. One APK. No root, no Shizuku, no companion app, no computer: the phone sets itself up.

<b>How it works.</b> Android's voice-call audio source carries both sides of a call, but only system identities may read it, ADB's shell user among them. AM Call Assistant embeds an ADB client and pairs with the phone's own Wireless debugging over 127.0.0.1. Nothing leaves the phone.

A short ADB session starts a shell daemon that writes each call to a file; the app is never on the audio path. Connections are authenticated both ways.

<b>Recordings</b> are Ogg Opus files, about 12 MB an hour, in the phone's Recordings/AM Call Assistant folder. A recording starts at the answer; a call nobody answered is not kept. Automatic mode records every call; ask-first records nothing until you tap Record. Messenger calls (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Viber) and Google Meet can be recorded too, both sides, app by app, Android 14+; all but Signal tested. It warns of a silent or cut-short recording, a recorder down, a call not recorded, or a setting in its way.

<b>Playing, finding, keeping.</b> A call plays as its waveform, both sides in both ears, through the earpiece at your ear, and on with the screen off. A Calls tab shows each call: its recording, or why not. Deleted recordings wait 30 days in Recently deleted; auto-delete clears old and short ones. Keep only some people's calls; name files with the caller or number.

<b>Three looks</b>: Memo; Deck, a pocket recorder; Multitrack, a sound editor - each light, dark or as the phone.

<b>Tasker</b>: two Plugin &gt; AM Call Assistant actions - Toggle AM Call Assistant, and AM Answering Machine with a typed, previewable greeting. Defaults use app values; Revert restores them.

<b>Answering machine</b>, off until you switch it on: it answers phone calls you don't take, plays your greeting - recorded or typed and spoken by the phone - your microphone muted, and keeps the caller's message, by the same rules as recording; you can still take the call. It pauses for a day after an emergency call. Experimental: checks and a test call say if it works on yours.

<b>Setup</b> takes two things by hand, once: turn on Developer options, then USB and Wireless debugging (one more switch on ColorOS, OxygenOS, realme UI), and type the pairing code into AM Call Assistant's notification. The pairing survives reboots, and Android's 7-day limit if you let it; the recorder restarts on the next Wi-Fi if the phone lets AM Call Assistant run on its own - setup asks; AM Call Assistant warns if it is stopped.

<b>What stays on.</b> USB and Wireless debugging stay on while AM Call Assistant is on: the recorder needs them. For banking apps, turning it off can hide Developer options or turn all debugging off. While debugging is on, a paired computer can connect on the same Wi-Fi. To stop for good, uninstall AM Call Assistant. Start without Wi-Fi opens port 5555 to run with no Wi-Fi until a reboot.

<b>Requirements.</b> Android 12+, and a phone whose audio hardware hands over the voice-call source. Both sides recorded on an Honor Magic 8 Pro, Galaxy S20, S23, M34 and a OnePlus on LineageOS. Settings → Diagnostics has a self-test.

<b>Recording calls is your responsibility.</b> Consent law differs by country and state. This app records what you tell it to and warns nobody on your behalf.

<b>What it does not ask for:</b> no RECORD_AUDIO, no phone state. INTERNET is needed for any TCP socket, even to 127.0.0.1, the only address used. READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (on Android 12, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) is asked only to list recordings made before a reinstall. READ_CALL_LOG and READ_CONTACTS are optional: they name callers. MANAGE_ONGOING_CALLS tells AM Call Assistant when a call ends. Notification access hides Android's debugging notification or names a messenger caller.
